# Skins and Collections

## Collections

Seasonal and collection cards are grouped by the middle two digits of the special card ID.

- `01`: Season 1
- `02`: Season 2
- `99`: GG collection

Each season introduces exclusive special cards and may also include skinned variants.

## Collector Packs

- **Collector** guarantees at least one skinned Special.
- **Collector XL** guarantees at least two skinned Specials.
- Non-collector packs do not include skinned cards.
