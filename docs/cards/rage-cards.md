# Rage Cards

Rage cards have negative effects that will challenge players in specific rounds.

## List of Rage cards

| NAME                 | IMAGE            | DESCRIPTION                                           |
|----------------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Broken Hearts        | ![](./img/effect/401.png) | Heart suited cards will not score during this level     |
| Broken Clubs         | ![](./img/effect/402.png) | Club suited cards will not score during this level      |
| Broken Diamonds      | ![](./img/effect/403.png) | Diamond suited cards will not score during this level   |
| Broken Spades        | ![](./img/effect/404.png) | Spade suited cards will not score during this level     |
| Broken Jokers        | ![](./img/effect/405.png) | Jokers will not score during this level                 |
| Diminished Hold      | ![](./img/effect/406.png) | Your hand will be reduced by 2 cards during this level  |
| Zero Waste           | ![](./img/effect/407.png) | You have no discards during this level                 |
| Unexpected Sacrifice | ![](./img/effect/408.png) | One of your special cards got sacrificed               |
| Strategic Quartet    | ![](./img/effect/409.png) | You can only play up to 4 cards during this level       |
| Dynamic Tactics      | ![](./img/effect/410.png) | The same hand cannot be played twice                   |
| Balanced Trade-off   | ![](./img/effect/411.png) | A poker play is worth double, another half             |
| Broken Figures       | ![](./img/effect/412.png) | Figures (Jack, Queen, and King) will not score         |
| Broken Modifiers     | ![](./img/effect/413.png) | Modifiers will not work during this level              |
| Last Words           | ![](./img/effect/414.png) | Silence the most repeated card in the deck             |

| Perpetual Curse      | ![](./img/effect/418.png) | Silence a random card in each hand played              |
