# Rage Cards

Rage cards are temporary debuffs applied during rage encounters. Their effects only last for that round unless a specific card states otherwise.

## Distribution in Classic Mod

- `C` tier: 45% probability, 500 reward, power cost 1
- `B` tier: 25% probability, 750 reward, power cost 2
- `A` tier: 20% probability, 1000 reward, power cost 3
- `S` tier: 10% probability, 1500 reward, power cost 4

## Special Rules

- `Locked Plays`, `Punched Ticket`, and `Blackout` cannot coexist in the same rage round.
- `Blackout` only appears on the Level 3 final boss rage.
- The final boss rage always uses a larger power budget than intermediate rage nodes.
