# Rage Cards

Rage cards have negative effects that will challenge players in specific rounds.

| ------------------ | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Broken Hearts      | ![401](./img/effect/401.png) | Heart suited cards will not score during this level              |
| Broken Clubs       | ![402](./img/effect/402.png) | Club suited cards will not score during this level               |
| Broken Diamonds    | ![403](./img/effect/403.png) | Diamond suited cards will not score during this level            |
| Broken Spades      | ![404](./img/effect/404.png) | Spade suited cards will not score during this level              |
| Broken Jokers      | ![405](./img/effect/405.png) | Jokers will not score during this level                          |
| Diminished Hold    | ![406](./img/effect/406.png) | Your hand will be reduced by 2 cards during this level           |
| Zero Waste         | ![407](./img/effect/407.png) | You have no discards during this level                           |
| Strategic Quartet  | ![409](./img/effect/409.png) | You can only play up to 4 cards during this level                |
| Broken Figures     | ![412](./img/effect/412.png) | Figures (Jack, Queen, and King) will not score                   |
| The Hand Leech     | ![416](./img/effect/416.png) | 2 hands less to play                                             |
| Double Trouble     | ![420](./img/effect/420.png) | You have to make double points in this round                     |
| Betraying the Weak | ![421](./img/effect/421.png) | Cards with a value of 5 or less will not score during this level |
