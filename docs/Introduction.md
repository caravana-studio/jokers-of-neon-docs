# Introduction

:::info Looking for a Simpler Overview?
If you are a content creator, partner, or just want a faster high-level overview of the game, take a look at our [Presskit](https://jokersofneon.com/presskit).
These docs are meant to be a detailed reference and go deep into the systems, rules, and progression behind Jokers of Neon.
:::

Jokers of Neon is a roguelike deck-builder fully on-chain card game built around poker hands, deck building, and escalating score checks.

Every run asks you to:

- Clear rounds by reaching target scores.
- Improve your deck through shops, packs, loot boxes, modifiers, specials, and power-ups.
- Choose a route across a branching map and adapt to the cards and challenges offered in that run.

Outside of runs, the game also includes a meta progression layer:

- Seasonal progression with free and premium reward tracks.
- Daily missions and level clears that grant XP.
- Daily, weekly, season, and tournament competition ladders.
- Seasonal card collections, skins, and limited collector content.
