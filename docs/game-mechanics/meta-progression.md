# Meta Progression

## Seasons

Jokers of Neon runs on a seasonal structure.

- Seasons last about **6 weeks**.
- Each season ends with a tournament that runs for about **10 days**.
- Seasons add exclusive special cards, reward tracks, and limited collector content.

## XP Sources

XP mainly comes from repeatable and run-based progression.

- **Daily missions**
  - Easy: `10 XP`
  - Medium: `20 XP`
  - Hard: `30 XP`
- **Level clears**

Daily missions reset around **06:00 UTC**.

## Season Progression

Each season has:

- A **free** reward track
- A **premium** reward track unlocked by the **Season Pass**
- Milestone rewards including packs and tournament entries

## Season Pass and Lives

Without Season Pass:

- Max lives: `3`
- Life recharge: `1 every 8 hours`

With Season Pass:

- Max lives: `6`
- Life recharge: `1 every 4 hours`
- Premium reward lane unlocked
- `20%` discount on season packs

One life is consumed each time you start a new run.

## Daily Mission Themes

Mission pools cover multiple playstyles, including:

- Score goals
- Deck building
- Rage encounters
- Poker hand goals
- Shop and economy actions

## Achievements

The game also tracks a persistent achievement layer.

- Total achievements: **32**
- Categories include deck building, scoring, rage rounds, power-ups, loot boxes, joker collection, hand types, progression, and slot management.
- Some achievements stay hidden until unlocked.
