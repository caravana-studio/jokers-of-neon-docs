# The Shop

The shop is where runs turn from survival into scaling. After clearing progression nodes, you can spend cash on deck quality, scoring upgrades, economy tools, and random reward products.

![608](./img/map.png)

In the shop, players can:

- Level up their plays to increase scoring potential.
- Purchase cards, including special cards, modifiers, traditional cards, and neon cards.
- Unlock slots for equipping more special cards.
- Buy power-ups, which can be used at any time.
- Open loot boxes that grant random cards.
- Burn unwanted cards from the deck to streamline strategy.

## Store Types

- **Pack store**: Basic, Advanced, Epic, and Legendary packs
- **Collector store**: limited-time Collector and Collector XL packs with skinned specials
- **Specials store**: buy and sell special cards
- **Modifiers store**: buy modifiers that alter suit or card type
- **Power-up store**: buy immediate-use point and multiplier boosts
- **Loot box store**: buy themed loot boxes by suit or card type

### Notable Loot Box Odds

- Basic loot box: `88%` traditional, `4%` Joker, `1%` Neon Joker, `5%` modifier, `2%` special
- Advanced loot box: `76%` traditional, `8%` Joker, `2%` Neon Joker, `10%` modifier, `4%` special
- Specials loot box: `25%` special, `15%` modifier, `60%` traditional
- Neon loot box: `97%` neon, `3%` Neon Joker

Suit-specific loot boxes also guarantee the Ace of their suit and then fill the remaining slots from a themed suit pool.

## Shop Pricing

- Special slot base price: `600`
- Slot prices double per purchase: `600, 1200, 2400, 4800, 9600, 19200`
- Burn base price: `100`
- Burn price grows by `+100` each time
- Traditional card: `200`
- Neon traditional card: `700`
- Joker: `1500`
- Neon Joker: `3000`

## Round Rewards

- Base reward per cleared round: `500 cash`
- Remaining discard bonus: `150 cash` each
- Remaining play bonus: `150 cash` each

## Discount System

The shop can apply discounts across these categories:

- Traditional cards
- Modifiers
- Specials
- Loot boxes
- Poker hands
- Burn
- Slot
- Power-ups

Current rules:

- Max discounts per shop visit: `3`
- Discount tries: `10`
- No discount: `70%`
- `B` discount: `20% off`, `15%`
- `A` discount: `30% off`, `10%`
- `S` discount: `50% off`, `5%`

## Reroll

Players start a run with **1 reroll**. Rerolls refresh the current shop inventory.
