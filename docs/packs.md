# Packs

## Pack Types

Player-facing pack families:

- Basic
- Advanced
- Epic
- Legendary
- Collector
- Collector XL

Current regular season packs are:

- Advanced
- Epic
- Legendary

Collector and Collector XL packs only appear during limited availability windows.

## Collector Rules

- Collector guarantees at least one skinned Special.
- Collector XL guarantees at least two skinned Specials.
- Non-collector packs do not include skinned cards.
